## About countdown pomodoro

countdown pomodoro is an Android timer app focused on short, deliberate work sessions and simple countdown timing. It combines Pomodoro-style pacing with straightforward time tracking so you can stay aware of how long each task takes without extra clutter.

The app is designed for people who want a lightweight productivity helper that keeps attention on the task at hand. Its minimalist layout also makes it a good fit for e-ink displays and other setups where clear, high-contrast timing information matters.
